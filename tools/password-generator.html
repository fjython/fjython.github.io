FAQ

Are passwords generated on a server?

No. Passwords are generated locally with the Web Crypto API (crypto.getRandomValues). Nothing is sent to a server.

What makes a strong password?

Use at least 12–16 characters and mix uppercase, lowercase, digits, and symbols. Avoid reusing passwords across sites.

Can I exclude ambiguous characters?

Yes. Turn on “Exclude ambiguous” to remove look-alikes such as 0/O and 1/l/I from the character set.
